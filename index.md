I am a Senior Research Scientist at [Sword Health](https://swordhealth.com/), and a PhD student at Instituto Superior Técnico and Instituto de Telecomunicações in Lisbon, advised by Prof. André Martins and Ricardo Rei.
I'm currently working on building frontier LLMs for healthcare with a focus on applications for mental health, and I'm particularly interested in the automatic evaluation of such models.
Previously, I worked on the development and evaluation of multilingual LLMs at Unbabel, and fairness-aware ML for fraud detection at Feedzai.

## News

- **Oct 2026** Released [MindEval2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22314), which pairs interpretable rubrics developed with clinical psychologists with a more realistic patient simulator that keeps memory across sessions. [Code and data](https://github.com/SWORDHealth/mind-eval).
- **Apr 2026** [Self-preference bias in rubric-based evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06996), showing that LLM judges favor their own outputs even when rubrics are objectively verifiable, accepted at **COLM 2026**. [Code](https://github.com/zeppombal/rubric-self-preference).
- **Nov 2025** Released [MindEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18491), a framework built with licensed clinical psychologists for evaluating LLMs in realistic, multi-turn mental health conversations, accepted at the **DAIH workshop @ COLM 2026**. Code and data are on [GitHub](https://github.com/SWORDHealth/mind-eval), and there's a [blog post with a video](https://sword.com/newsroom/sword-introduces-mindeval).
- **Jul 2025** Joined [Sword Health](https://swordhealth.com/) as a Senior Research Scientist.
- **2023–2025** Joined [Unbabel](https://unbabel.com/) as a Research Scientist in March 2023, working on post-training and automatic evaluation of multilingual LLMs, with a focus on machine translation. Papers include [Tower](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17733) (COLM 2024 spotlight), [Tower v2](https://aclanthology.org/2024.wmt-1.12/) and [TowerChat](https://aclanthology.org/2024.wmt-1.100/) (winners of three WMT24 shared tasks), [Tower+](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1366/) (ACL 2026), [M-Prometheus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04953) and [Zero-shot Benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01001) (COLM 2025), and TACL papers on [metric interference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08327) and [context-aware translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04205). Open models: [Tower](https://huggingface.co/collections/Unbabel/tower-659eaedfe36e6dd29eb1805c), [Tower+](https://huggingface.co/collections/Unbabel/tower-plus-6846ca452a10c0905dc03c0f), [M-Prometheus](https://huggingface.co/collections/Unbabel/m-prometheus-67f3b17e6409b2550b698822).
